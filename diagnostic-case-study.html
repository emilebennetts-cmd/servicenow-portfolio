01 / The client's pain & history

In a representative enterprise environment, discovery questions, configuration observations and remediation decisions can become fragmented across spreadsheets and workshops. Without provenance, teams struggle to explain why a finding was raised, which evidence supports it and whether a proposed fix was approved. This scenario is illustrative, not a verified claim about a named customer.

03 / Architectural best practices

Least privilege

Prefer offline review and authorised read-only collection. No background remediation or customer-instance write is implied.

OOTB-first

Assess native ServiceNow capability before customising; keep application scope, upgrade path and deployment controls explicit.

Traceability

Connect findings to originating questions, collector outputs, rule versions and human review decisions.

Release discipline

Separate source tests, DEV installation evidence, acceptance tests and production sign-off.
