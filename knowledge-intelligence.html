Diagnostic Workbench 2 / Companion architecture

Knowledge Intelligence.
Governed by evidence.

A separately deployed companion package that connects authorised knowledge sources, diagnostic questions, read-only evidence, rule evaluation and reviewable remediation patterns.

DEV verification reported in source repository · not independently reproduced

01 / The client's pain & history

Disconnected knowledge

Illustrative enterprise problem: articles, troubleshooting scripts and findings are scattered across teams. Investigators cannot easily trace why a recommendation was raised or whether its source is authorised for reuse.

Premature recommendations

Without evidence lineage and approval gates, a plausible hypothesis can be mistaken for a validated finding. The design separates evidence capture from human confirmation.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

01 · Govern

Register licensed sources and classify articles; no ServiceNow KB articles are bundled.

02 · Discover

Map symptoms to questions and read-only collectors.

03 · Evaluate

Capture structured evidence, apply rules and retain metric-level provenance.

04 · Review

Findings await human review before client-facing publication or solution-pattern use.

Verification and licensing

Evidence classification: package design and inventory are source-only; September DEV installation is repository-reported DEV; independent current DEV and production verification are not established. No live screenshots or production usage are claimed.

The static portfolio needs no ServiceNow licence. Deploying the scoped companion requires suitable ServiceNow application and role entitlements. Access to underlying licensed ITSM, ITOM, SecOps, IRM, HRSD, CSM or ITAM data and any external knowledge-provider connector must be reviewed against product subscriptions, API permissions and content redistribution rights. No IntegrationHub or AI entitlement is implied by the static presentation.
