1. The client's pain & history

Consulting delivery can fragment across workshop notes, requirements spreadsheets, design drafts, build tasks and handover packs. Without controlled links between these records, teams repeat discovery, lose decisions and struggle to demonstrate what was approved, built and tested.

Fragmented discovery

Requirements are hard to trace to evidence and decisions.

Inconsistent reuse

Patterns are selected without clear scope and review gates.

Unclear acceptance

Draft documents and demonstrations can be mistaken for approved delivery.

2. The ServiceNow solution

The Workbench source connects governed scope selection, linked workshops and requirements, candidate patterns, Delivery Plan checkpoints, document generation and handover. Generated material remains draft until reviewed.

3. Architectural best practices

OOTB-first

Preserve native ServiceNow approvals, ITIL-aligned Change authority and upgrade-safe scoped design.

Evidence-driven gates

Separate source code, synthetic demo, historic DEV read-back and approved runtime acceptance.

Controlled generation

Draft documents link back to their source; generation does not grant approval or deployment authority.
