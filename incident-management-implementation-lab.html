1. The client's pain & history

In this fictional organisation, users report outages by email, chat and phone. One team logs incidents, another receives unmanaged transfers, and priority is guessed from the caller’s seniority rather than business impact and urgency. On-hold tickets lack reasons, operational notes leak into customer communications, and closure is recorded without clear proof that service was restored. This is an illustrative consulting scenario, not a customer deployment assertion.

2. The ServiceNow solution

Build on the native incident record and its standard task model. Route genuine service interruptions through logging, categorisation, impact/urgency-based prioritisation, assignment, diagnosis, restoration and verified closure. Keep a service request on the Catalog path, an underlying cause on Problem and a disruptive fix on authorised Change, rather than overloading Incident with bespoke tables.

OOTB implementation sequence

  1. Verify the installed Incident lifecycle, roles, assignment rules, notifications, forms and existing customisations in the target instance.
  2. Confirm intake channels, duplicate detection, mandatory fields, assignment group ownership and impacted CI/business service data quality.
  3. Validate the instance-configured impact and urgency priority data lookup rather than hard-coding a universal P1 matrix.
  4. Configure and validate SLA definitions, pause conditions, schedules, escalation and user-facing communication separately.
  5. Test approved resolution, reopening, closure and linkage to Knowledge, Problem, Change and Major Incident pathways as applicable.

3. Architectural best practices & verification

Testing realities

Positive and negative personas must prove record creation, work-note segregation, assignment, approval restrictions and direct-link ACL denial. SLA results need an explicit schedule, pause and timezone baseline. Resolve and Close state behaviour must be read from the actual installed release and configuration.

Escalation and Major Incident features are independently scoped; an ordinary incident should not be presented as major-incident acceptance. Do not infer service impact from a weak or duplicate CMDB identifier.

Licensing and entitlements

The static public lab and CSV export require no ServiceNow licence. Native Incident Management requires applicable ITSM/fulfiller entitlements and authorised itil or sn_incident_write roles subject to actual ACLs. Service Operations Workspace, Major Incident playbooks, ITOM Event Management, IntegrationHub, ATF, Virtual Agent, analytics and Now Assist need target-specific product, role and subscription checks.

No custom table, IntegrationHub spoke or live provider is provisioned by this lab.
