01 / The client's pain & history

Dashboards became trusted before data collection was trusted.

Picture a fictional estate with ageing discovery sources, incomplete CI ownership and inconsistent data standards. Different teams cite the CMDB Health headline as evidence of improvement, but no one has reconciled the calculation time, evaluated class population or underlying rule versions.

An apparent score gain might reflect a newly excluded class rather than a resolved data-quality issue. A missing or disabled scheduled job can leave an older score in view, while duplicate identities and relationship gaps remain unresolved.

The core control

Score provenance precedes score improvement.

A defensible operating model records job state, calculation completion, class/service scope, failed-CI populations, rule versions, accountable owners and independently reviewed post-fix results.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

Use native health signals, then govern their interpretation.

CMDB Health covers Completeness (required and recommended fields), Correctness (including duplicate, stale and orphan rules), Compliance (audit/certification controls) and separate Relationship health. Relevant scheduled calculations and relationship processors require configuration and confirmed execution. The new dashboard's calculation approach is release-dependent; never substitute the arithmetic in this mock for a native score.

    03 / Architectural best practices

    Preserve platform semantics and upgrades.

    OOTB first

    Do not rewrite calculation scripts

    Start with CMDB Health preferences, effective KPI jobs, CI Class Manager rules, IRE and native metrics; avoid custom script modifications without change control.

    Scale

    Bound class and job scope

    On large CI estates, control schedule overlap and data growth. Document metric failure-limit effects and distinguish incomplete calculations from actual zero failures.

    Assurance

    Separate test and release authority

    Verify read/write permissions, negative-persona denial, provenance and rollback before approving remediation at scale.

    ServiceNow licensing / entitlement review

    The public static lab and offline CSV need no ServiceNow licence. Native CMDB Health access requires the appropriate CMDB capabilities and sn_cmdb_admin or admin for job configuration; validate installed applications, user roles and contracted rights for the target release. Discovery/ITOM Visibility, Service Graph Connectors, CMDB 360, Performance Analytics, ATF, IntegrationHub and AI features require separate licence or activation checks where used.
