      03 / Architectural best practices

      OOTB-first controls, least privilege, upgrade-safe evidence.

      Evidence identity before scoring

      Version-pin collector and target application; require compatible scope, definitions, read-only declaration, comparable denominator and freshness. Do not turn an inaccessible table into a zero defect rate. Re-run only with written client authorisation.

      Separation of duties

      Collectors observe; rules propose; an engagement lead confirms or dismisses findings. A Delivery Workbench pattern recommendation is not approval to run a fix script, import an update set or change production.

      Release & replay discipline

      Prefer native CMDB Health, IRE, CMDB 360 and ITSM controls where licensed. Require unique evidence identifiers, reproducible calculations, ACL negative tests, valid rollback plans and current-version DEV/UAT before promotion.

      Catalogue provenance

      Diagnostic Workbench 2 source describes 20 collector module packs, 42 rules and four cross-module correlations. The separately deployed Knowledge Intelligence companion documents 45 read-only collectors. These are distinct catalogues; their counts must not be added or presented as installed.
