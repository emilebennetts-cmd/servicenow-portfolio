01 / The client's pain & history

Findings disappear between assessment and implementation.

In a representative enterprise ServiceNow estate, diagnostic teams identify configuration drift, weak ownership and recurring operational exceptions. Delivery teams then reconstruct the context from slide decks, tickets and meetings. Requirements lose their originating evidence; a successful demo is mistaken for production acceptance. This scenario is illustrative and does not describe an identified client.

03 / Architectural best practices

Integrate through contracts, not assumptions.

OOTB-first

Use native task, change, knowledge and CMDB capabilities where licensed and appropriate. Custom scoped tables should add only the governance fields that standard records cannot support.

Safe integration

Prefer supported REST APIs or IntegrationHub spokes with explicit authentication, idempotency keys, error queues, rate limits and field-level ACL checks. A proposed interface is not a verified integration.

Release assurance

Apply ITIL 4 change enablement, test evidence, approval separation and rollback readiness. Record target environment, application version, tester and timestamp before asserting verification.
