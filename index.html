Universal Delivery Workbench

The client's pain & history

Consulting delivery often fragments discovery notes, requirements, solution patterns, approval decisions and handover evidence across disconnected documents and tools. That makes traceability and repeatability difficult.

The ServiceNow solution

The documented workbench unifies the delivery journey: scoped discovery, governed pattern selection, requirements, design, delivery planning, controlled documents, approval evidence, testing and handover.

Architectural best practices

Preserve stable scope and record identities, gate reusable patterns by review state, keep evidence and approvals traceable, and separate local workflow capabilities from unverified third-party connector activation.

Case study 02 · x_1577958_dw2

Diagnostic Workbench 2

The client's pain & history

Platform investigations can become dependent on ad hoc queries, disconnected knowledge and inconsistent evidence. Without a repeatable diagnostic method, support teams struggle to explain findings and validate remediation.

The ServiceNow solution

The repository contains Now SDK application source plus a versioned Knowledge Intelligence companion package. Its documented components include governed knowledge schemas, rule/finding logic, offline validation and 45 read-only collectors.

Architectural best practices

Keep diagnostic collection read-only by default, separate evidence collection from remediation, validate rules offline, and deploy only through authorised non-production testing and controlled promotion.

Engineering principles

Designed for platform longevity

OOTB-first, extensions where justified

Prefer native ServiceNow workflows, data contracts and controls. Introduce custom scope only where a clear capability gap and upgrade strategy exist.

Evidence and governance

Maintain traceability from source observations to decisions, requirements, test evidence and operational acceptance.

Security and integration boundaries

Enforce roles and ACLs, keep credentials in platform-owned stores, and distinguish mocked adapters from authorised live integrations.

Licensing and entitlements

This static portfolio requires no ServiceNow licence. Live demonstrations may require a developer instance and the relevant application, IntegrationHub or spoke entitlements.
