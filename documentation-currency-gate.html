Licensing and entitlement dependencies

Public static review requires no ServiceNow licence. Native Workbench testing needs authorised custom-app hosting, roles and ACLs; ITSM/CMDB, ITOM Discovery and Service Graph, IntegrationHub/spokes, ATF, Performance Analytics, AI and any SecOps, IRM, CSM, HRSD or ITAM capability require checks against the target contract and instance where invoked.

This public review is designed to demonstrate governance, not to assert a customer's subscription or permissions.
