Architecture and verification boundaries

What this checks

PNG signature, per-chunk CRC-32, safe canvas chunk types and ordering, bounded IHDR dimensions, SHA-256 of actual bytes and strict v1 receipt fields. This is structural validation, not full pixel decoding. A changed pixel or any other byte produces a digest mismatch.

What remains gated

Image-by-image customer and owner approval, visual redaction inspection, runtime source/instance evidence, deployment identity and screenshot captions. Genuine Workbench image candidates are still HOLD.

Evidence classification: SOURCE-ONLY — this verifier's public source; MOCK-ONLY — synthetic regression fixtures; DEV-VERIFIED — historical, version-scoped platform evidence only; PRODUCTION-VERIFIED — not established.
