Evidence governance / Owner-side preparation

Turn a screenshot candidate into a reviewable handoff.

A practical, offline-first checklist for the eight inspected Workbench evidence records. Prepare a non-sensitive JSON review packet without uploading an image, disclosing private source references or granting publication permission.

Publication HOLD is enforced by design. This tool cannot confirm ownership, obtain legal consent, inspect an original image or approve its release. Document-source records are not application screenshots. Completing every box only creates a handoff for an independent, authorised reviewer. Zero genuine Workbench screenshots are approved for this public portfolio.

03 / Controlled progression, not automatic promotion
Prepare privately
Use the browser-local redaction desk on a permitted copy. Retain the original and sanitised derivative only in authorised storage.
Verify independently
Use the PNG and receipt verifier. Hash agreement is byte-integrity evidence, not proof of ownership or instance execution.
Approve outside this site
An authorised reviewer records an explicit approval privately. Only then may a reviewed derivative and checksum be added to the public publication allowlist via the gated release process.


Licensing and technical boundaries
The static checklist, redaction desk, receipt verifier and evidence gallery require no ServiceNow licence. Recreating or validating live Workbench screens may require App Engine/custom application rights, platform roles and ACLs, plus contract-specific ITSM/MIM, CMDB/ITOM, IntegrationHub, Performance Analytics, AI or third-party provider entitlements. A local browser test never establishes DEV-VERIFIED or PRODUCTION-VERIFIED status.
