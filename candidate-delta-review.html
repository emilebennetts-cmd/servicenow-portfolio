    03 / Architectural best practices

    The two candidates are checked against the 29 planned case IDs, titles, workstreams and personas from the same public acceptance catalogue. The validator rejects extra schema fields, unsupported statuses, forged verification flags, unsafe evidence aliases, inconsistent evidence-missing lists and invalid context formats. A failure never generates a delta.

    A status transition is a reported change, not an execution result. A removed alias, observed failure, blocked case, or context mismatch deserves further review. Any authorised DEV acceptance requires witnessed native runs, retained private proof, target/version pinning, role and ACL validation, and separate release approval. Production claims require further independently verified production evidence.

    Licensing: This static comparison tool requires no ServiceNow licence. Native implementation or testing may depend on App Engine/custom-table entitlements, ITSM, ATF availability, roles and ACLs, and applicable ITOM, IntegrationHub, analytics, AI or external-provider rights under the target contract.
