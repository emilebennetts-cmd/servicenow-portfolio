From fragmented operations to governed delivery

Two complementary engineering products: one establishes traceable diagnostic evidence; the other guides a controlled consulting delivery lifecycle. These case studies describe documented architecture, not independently witnessed production outcomes.

01 / Diagnostic engineering

Diagnostic Workbench 2

Client pain & history

Operational assessments often rely on manual exports, inconsistent findings and fragmented evidence. Repeating an investigation can become difficult when the inputs and rules are not recorded together.

The ServiceNow solution

A scoped workbench and separately packaged Knowledge Intelligence component organise read-only collection, rule evaluation and governed findings. The companion package is documented as separate from the main UI navigation.

CollectValidateEvaluateReview

Architectural best practices

Keep evidence provenance distinct from interpretation; avoid equating a successful source build with installed functionality. Installation and promotion require authorised non-production verification and change gates.

Evidence and verification checklist
  • Source: Now SDK application and Knowledge Intelligence companion package.
  • Check collector output is read-only and sanitised.
  • Re-run offline validation and capture result logs.
  • Verify UI navigation separately from companion Classic modules.
  • Record target instance, version, persona and test timestamp before marking DEV-verified.

02 / Delivery engineering

Universal Delivery Workbench

Client pain & history

Discovery notes, reusable patterns, delivery plans and acceptance evidence can drift across disconnected tools. This increases rework and obscures which decisions were actually approved.

The ServiceNow solution

A guided lifecycle connects discovery, scope selection, requirements, controlled pattern reuse, design, build, testing and handover. Draft generation preserves review requirements rather than treating generated outputs as approved implementations.

DiscoverDesignBuildAssureHandover

Architectural best practices

Prefer OOTB capabilities where suitable, retain traceable decisions and separate candidate patterns from certified reusable assets. Optional provider integrations remain gated by connection and entitlement verification.

Evidence and verification checklist
  • Source: lifecycle, catalogue and governance descriptions in public repository.
  • Test draft generation without overwriting reviewed answers.
  • Confirm pattern eligibility and target-specific validation.
  • Run persona, approval, negative-access and document tests in an authorised instance.
  • Do not infer live Jira, Confluence or Notion sync from mock contracts.

Classification: SOURCE-ONLY for current described features; prior DEV read-back is historical and version-specific.
