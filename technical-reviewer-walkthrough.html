1. The client's pain & history

ServiceNow delivery programmes often accumulate disconnected workshop notes, configuration decisions, reusable patterns and handover artefacts. Platform operations teams separately investigate incidents, CMDB data quality and knowledge gaps. Without a controlled handoff, the same finding is retyped across delivery records, losing provenance and approval context. This is a representative problem statement, not an assertion about a named customer's environment.

3. Architectural best practices

OOTB first, governed extensions second

Use standard ServiceNow task, approval, knowledge and CMDB capabilities where suitable. Scope custom records, reusable patterns and generated artefacts with explicit ownership, ACLs and upgrade-safe integration contracts. The source design is not a claim of OOTB certification.

Versioned evidence and release gates

Keep build identity, target scope, environment, reviewer and test receipts together. A historical DEV-reported baseline cannot validate newer source or a different application identity. External writes remain disabled until authorised connection and entitlement testing.
