Diagnostic intelligence, without unsafe automation.

Diagnostic Workbench 2 combines a guided assessment journey, read-only evidence collection, rule-based findings and human-controlled remediation. The Knowledge Intelligence companion adds governed knowledge-to-evidence traceability.

01 · The client's pain & history

Platform assessments often begin with scattered scripts, inconsistent symptoms, unrepeatable findings and recommendations disconnected from measured evidence. Teams need a repeatable way to investigate without granting a diagnostic interface unrestricted write access to customer instances. This is the engineering problem addressed by the workbench; it is not a claim about a particular customer's deployment.

Separation of deployment

03 · Architectural best practices

Least privilege

Keep collectors read-only, aggregate-only and explicitly authorised. Refuse consumer-level data and secrets at intake. Review scripts before execution in client environments.

Human accountability

Correlations suggest investigation paths, not proven causation. Findings and solution patterns remain draft until reviewed; no automatic remediation is implied.

Upgrade-conscious delivery

Preserve scoped application boundaries, source-controlled catalogs, separate companion package release evidence and controlled update-set promotion.

ITIL 4 alignment

Link diagnosis to incident/problem investigation and controlled change enablement, retaining authoritative ServiceNow records rather than replacing native workflows.

Licensing and entitlements

This static portfolio page requires no ServiceNow licence. Deployment requires authorised instance access and applicable custom-application entitlements. Diagnostic collectors may query ITSM, ITOM, CMDB, SecOps, IRM, CSM, HRSD and ITAM records only where the organisation has relevant subscriptions and access. Licensed knowledge sources and optional integration services require separate rights and approvals.
