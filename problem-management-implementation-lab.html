1. The client's pain & history

In this illustrative organisation, a shared infrastructure fault caused recurring tickets across the service desk. Teams restored service repeatedly but recorded no single accountable investigation. Symptoms, suspect CIs, workarounds and changes lived in disconnected records; customers received inconsistent guidance, and leadership mistook incident closure for root-cause elimination.

2. The ServiceNow solution

Use native Problem Management to distinguish incident restoration from underlying cause. Coordinate investigation through Problem Tasks, communicate approved workarounds, optionally link a known-error knowledge article and govern lasting remediation through Change Management. Link CMDB/service context only where the identifiers and ownership are reliable.

OOTB implementation sequence

  1. Confirm installed ITSM capabilities, roles and the active Problem state model; existing and migrated instances may behave differently.
  2. Configure Problem ownership, assessment criteria, incident links, CI/service context and RCA Problem Task types.
  3. Document workaround communication, known-error prerequisites and positive/negative knowledge access.
  4. Connect proposed permanent fixes to native Change approval, implementation and rollback controls.
  5. Test recurrence monitoring, reanalysis, risk acceptance and closure with independent evidence.
