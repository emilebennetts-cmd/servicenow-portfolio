1. The client's pain & history

Multiple sources, competing owners

Discovery, cloud connectors and manual updates may reconcile the same CI differently. Without source authority, a newer value is not necessarily the correct owner.

A healthy CI without accountability

Completeness scores can coexist with missing business owners, stale attestations and ambiguous operational support. Incidents and change impact assessments then route to the wrong teams.

Approval mistaken for a checkbox

When the record owner also acts as its sole reviewer, governance loses independence. A completed worksheet is not proof of environment configuration or operational acceptance.

Representative enterprise situation, not a statement about any named customer or production environment.

2. The ServiceNow solution — ownership assurance before certification

Use the CMDB/CSDM relationship model and existing CI reference attributes for accountability, reconcile competing feeds through authoritative source policies and Identification and Reconciliation Engine controls, then route evidence for a human review. 3. Architectural best practices & licensing

OOTB-first

Prefer existing CMDB ownership references, CSDM service relationships, IRE reconciliation and native audit before creating custom ownership tables or an independent CI master.

Fail-closed lifecycle

Flag unmapped owners and evidence gaps, retain source provenance, avoid automatic re-ownership, and require role separation. Do not treat a simulation as an approval or a completed ATF run.

Entitlements are target-specific

Core CMDB/CSDM features require a suitably licensed ServiceNow environment and roles. ITOM Discovery, Service Graph Connectors, Service Mapping, CMDB Data Manager, Performance Analytics and custom app capabilities may require additional entitlements; confirm contract and plugin availability.
