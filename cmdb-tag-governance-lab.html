01 / The client's pain & history

Many cloud sources, no shared language.

A fictional multi-cloud estate ingests tags from cloud discovery, connectors and manual updates. Environment, env and Env may mean the same thing, while owner and cost-centre fields may be missing or contradictory. Chargeback reports and tag-based service populations then disagree. A mass clean-up performed solely in ServiceNow can be undone by the next authoritative discovery cycle.

Operational pain

Duplicate semantics

Different raw keys represent the same business attribute. Preserve original source and aliases before normalising.

Governance pain

Unowned exceptions

Policy failures remain unresolved when responsibility and evidence are not assigned to an accountable team.

Platform pain

Re-ingestion risk

Uncontrolled manual edits can be overwritten, misclassify a CI or alter application-service membership.

03 / Architectural best practices

Native policy first; preserve source authority.

OOTB first

Tag Governance policies

Confirm the plugin/app is installed and entitled. Use native Tag Count, Tag Presence and Tag Key & Value policies rather than bespoke bulk-fix scripts.

Source of truth

Discovery and connectors

Fix provider tags upstream where possible. Re-run Discovery/Service Graph ingestion and verify authoritative tag values, not just a one-time CMDB display.

Blast radius

Service Mapping

Review tag criteria and service-candidate membership before changing an environment key or value. Validate application-service impacts in authorised DEV.
