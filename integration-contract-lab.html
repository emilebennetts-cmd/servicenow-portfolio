01 / The client's pain & history

Why a green sync icon is not a control.

Split systems of record

Representational enterprise scenario: business analysis tracks requirements in ServiceNow while delivery teams also create Jira stories, Confluence pages and Notion decisions. No single tool knows which field owns the accepted answer.

Unreliable retries

A timeout after external creation can be mistaken for a failed write. A naïve retry creates duplicates. A remote edit during an outage can be silently overwritten by a stale local payload.

False connectivity

Historical sync logs and sample URLs do not establish that an approved connection works today. A green indicator without credentials, ACL checks and replay evidence is a risky release claim.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

Governed correlation rather than blind replication.

Source-reviewed Delivery Workbench design describes destination-bound correlation, source and external revisions, field ownership, reviewed batch/unlink, an explicit authority decision and error/replay controls. Live transport is withheld until validated connections and an atomic operation ledger exist.

03 / Architectural best practices

Seven gates before live activation.

G01 · IdentityCorrect application scope, release and source revision.Native target inventory and revision read-back.

OOTB-first delivery

Use supported ServiceNow integration patterns, REST Messages, Flow Designer/IntegrationHub where licensed, platform credential/connection aliases and native audit and ACL controls. Avoid hardcoded secrets, UI-driven bypasses and writing directly to approval state.

Licensing and entitlements

Static site: no ServiceNow licence. Native custom Workbench: confirm App Engine/custom-app and table allowances. Live provider integration: ServiceNow IntegrationHub/spoke or REST licensing as contracted, provider API accounts/permissions, ITSM product rights where applicable, approved outbound connections and platform roles.
