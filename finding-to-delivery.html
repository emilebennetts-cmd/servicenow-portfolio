One finding. A governed path to delivery.

Operational investigation and implementation delivery have different responsibilities. Explore a conceptual handoff between Diagnostic Workbench 2 and Universal Delivery Workbench, preserving provenance without creating a second writable evidence store.

01 / INVESTIGATE

Diagnostic Workbench 2

Owns investigation runs, operational findings and writable evidence. Knowledge Intelligence is a separately deployed companion package; its Classic modules are not yet wired into the local-first Workbench navigation.

Output: reviewed finding and a traceable source reference.

02 / DELIVER

Universal Delivery Workbench

Consumes source references for discovery, requirements, reusable patterns, design decisions, assurance and handover. The source describes governed reconciliation that preserves edited answers and approvals.

Output: reviewable delivery artefacts, not duplicated operational evidence.
