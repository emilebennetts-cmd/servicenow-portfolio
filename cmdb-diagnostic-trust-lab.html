02 / Evidence controls

What the status actually means

Acceptance logic

  • NOT ASSESSED: unavailable source cannot be treated as zero, PASS or FAIL.
  • PARTIAL: truncated grouped scan cannot establish a whole-population duplicate rate.
  • REVIEW: known duplicate, missing ownership, stale assets or invalid relationships require human triage.
  • PASS: only a complete, accessible synthetic fixture with no configured anomalies; not a production acceptance.
  • Relationship union: distinct incomplete records = missing parent + missing child − both missing. The subtraction prevents double-counting within this fixture.

What would constitute real proof

  • Approved release and instance, service account permissions and full query guard checks.
  • Reproducible ATF/DEV fixtures, expected/actual results, execution timestamps and collector/source SHA.
  • Permission-denied, missing-table, timeout and high-cardinality tests; evidence output allowlist.
  • Reconcile findings to OOTB CMDB Health, IRE rules, CI Class Manager and applicable CSDM.
  • Sanitised screenshots and reports require provenance, review and explicit publication approval.

Architectural best practices
OOTB-first and evidence-led
ServiceNow identification and reconciliation rules remain authoritative. The collector's role is to observe, describe its confidence, minimise exported data and initiate governed remediation — not update a CI or invent a green KPI when inputs fail.
Licensing & entitlements
No runtime entitlement implied
This static interactive lab requires no ServiceNow licence. Any actual collector requires approved platform access, applicable roles/ACLs and release-tested tables. CMDB Health, Discovery/ITOM Visibility, Service Graph connectors, App Engine and IntegrationHub are feature- and subscription-dependent; confirm customer entitlements before deploying functionality.
