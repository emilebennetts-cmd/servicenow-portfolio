01 / The client's pain & history

Two release lines can look deceptively alike.

A delivery team sees a working demonstration and a rich source library labelled “Universal Delivery Workbench.” But the app identities, update-set provenance and installed baselines differ. Promoting one cohort's test receipt to the other can hide missing tables, mismatched ACLs and unsupported upgrades.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

Make installation identity a release prerequisite.

Identify

Confirm target application and scope against the exact source lineage before installing or promoting metadata.

Reconcile

Pin the candidate commit, pack version, dependency manifest and transport method; detect stale revisions.

Verify

Test actual dictionary, scoped APIs, roles, ACL denials, negative personas and replay recovery in an authorised DEV/TST.

Approve

Obtain ITSM owner, security, release and platform decisions before deployment or evidence publication.

Interactive architecture review / local demonstration

Seven compatibility gates. No automatic promotion.

This worksheet is MOCK-ONLY: it is an offline, browser-local decision aid, not connected to ServiceNow. “Evidence supplied” is self-reported and cannot confer DEV-VERIFIED or PRODUCTION-VERIFIED status. It exports no customer identifiers or entered prose.

Application & scope identity

G01 · Blocker

Does the authorised target contain the exact intended application identity? Separate demo and source generations fail this check by default.

Pinned source & transport

G02

Is the commit, package version, update-set preview and source-to-target transport documented and non-destructive?

Entitlement and plugin readiness

G03

Are App Engine/custom application and table allowances, required ITSM features, integration rights and installed plugins confirmed?

Roles, ACLs and negative personas

G04

Have read, write, create, delete and cross-engagement denials been exercised using representative non-admin personas?

Version drift and idempotency

G05

Have stale-version rejection, concurrent generation, draft replay and duplicate prevention been tested on the same installed build?

Recovery, upgrade and performance

G06

Are rollback, interrupted execution, change collision, upgrade preview and reasonable load behaviour evidenced?

Business acceptance and release authority

G07

Have source-specific native ATF/UAT, operational ownership, privacy and Change/release approval been independently reviewed?
