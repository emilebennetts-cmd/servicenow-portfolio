01 / The client's pain & history

Fragmented operational evidence

In a typical legacy ServiceNow estate, diagnostic evidence can live across spreadsheets, instance exports, scripts and separate support queues. Without consistent provenance and review, teams struggle to distinguish observed defects from assumptions.

03 / Architectural best practices

OOTB-first and upgrade-safe

Use native ServiceNow workflows, scoped application boundaries, roles, ACLs and standard change/release governance where suitable. Preserve stable record identifiers, evidence references and approval gates; treat patterns as reviewed accelerators rather than universal drop-in implementations.

Explicit integration authority

Keep external writes disabled until connectors, credentials, data ownership, replay handling and conflict behaviour have been validated. Delivery source explicitly describes Jira, Confluence and Notion contracts as mock-only and live provider authentication as blocked.
