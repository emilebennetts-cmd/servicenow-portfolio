1. The client's pain & history

In this illustrative client scenario, instructions lived in old shared folders, specialist inboxes and obsolete knowledge articles. Service-desk agents reused conflicting fixes, new colleagues could not find trusted procedures, and owners had no disciplined review or retirement cycle. Knowledge was treated as a document dump rather than a governed service.

2. The ServiceNow solution

Use the native knowledge base and kb_knowledge article model to consolidate answers, govern contributions through ownership and user criteria, and apply the configured publish and retire processes. The goal is searchable, traceable knowledge, not a new custom article table.

Native build sequence

  1. Confirm Knowledge Management capabilities, active knowledge bases, target release and article versioning/Advanced plugin status.
  2. Define knowledge bases, managers, ownership groups, article templates and audience-specific Can Read / Can Contribute criteria.
  3. Choose the appropriate native approval-publish and retirement workflow/flow for the release; preserve existing upgrade-safe patterns.
  4. Validate author, approver, authorised reader and excluded reader personas using synthetic articles and clean sessions.
  5. Verify search, feedback and outdated/retired article behaviour; document test receipts privately before accepting any claim.
