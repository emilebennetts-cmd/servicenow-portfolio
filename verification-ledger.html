How a claim becomes verified

Source-only → DEV-verified

Record exact commit, app scope/version, authorised instance, deployment receipt, reproducible test procedure, timestamped result and reviewer. A source README asserting an installation remains “DEV-reported” until the underlying execution evidence is reviewed.

DEV-verified → production-verified

Require change approval, production deployment record, scoped permissions, independent read-back, acceptance tests, rollback plan and operational handover. Customer identities and credentials must never appear on this public site.

Screenshot candidate → approved public image

Confirm original file, capture date, application version, screen purpose and authorisation; redact or replace customer identifiers, personal data, credentials, instance addresses and confidential configuration. Review the final image itself before publication.

Licensing & entitlement dependencies

These static pages and browser-only demonstrations do not require ServiceNow licensing. Deploying custom scoped apps requires an authorised instance and the relevant application entitlements. Specific delivery patterns may additionally depend on licensed ITSM, ITOM, IRM, CSM, HRSD, IntegrationHub spokes, AI or analytics products. Live external integrations also require approved provider credentials and connection policies; entitlement requirements must be checked against the actual customer subscription.
