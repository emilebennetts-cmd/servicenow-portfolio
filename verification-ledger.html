Three verification gates before a real deployment claim

01 / Source identity

Trace exact source commit, scoped application, package/version and configuration dependencies. Generated patterns and synthetic fixtures are not installed configuration.

02 / Target execution

Obtain authorised DEV instance read-back, evidence of ACL/role behaviour, positive and negative ATF/UAT tests and reproducible logs. Historical DEV statements must not be treated as new validation.

03 / Promotion authority

Secure formal change approval, production read-back, operational acceptance and authorised, sanitised screenshots before external claims or publication.

Architectural & entitlement boundaries

Follow ITIL 4 control ownership and favour native ServiceNow processes before implementing custom scoped applications, REST actions or integrations. The portfolio and browser-local tools require no ServiceNow licence. Live Workbench deployment depends on applicable App Engine/custom-app rights, roles and ACLs. ITSM/MIM, CMDB/ITOM/Discovery, IntegrationHub, Performance Analytics/AI and third-party connector entitlements require product- and contract-specific validation. Image-publication rights are separate from all licences.
