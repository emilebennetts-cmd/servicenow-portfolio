ClassificationWhat it establishesCurrent boundary

DEV-VERIFIEDA dated, attributable development-instance check for a specified version.Historical evidence only; not current or production proof.

03 / Architectural best practices

OOTB-first and upgrade-safe

Prefer supported ServiceNow capabilities and configuration over unnecessary customisation. Apply ITIL 4 practices and explicit design decision records.

Controlled by design

Separate application scopes, apply least privilege, maintain auditability and require approval before promotion. A static portfolio page is not a live platform demonstration.

Licensing & entitlements

The static website requires no ServiceNow licence. Deploying custom Workbench applications may require App Engine entitlements under the customer's agreement. ITSM, ITOM, SecOps, IRM, HRSD, CSM, ITAM, IntegrationHub, Performance Analytics, AI features and third-party providers can have additional subscription, plugin or transaction dependencies. Validate the target contract and enabled products before delivery.
