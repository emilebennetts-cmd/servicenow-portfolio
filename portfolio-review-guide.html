03 / Architectural best practices

OOTB-first. Traceable. Upgradeable.

ITIL 4 and platform design

Use supported ServiceNow Incident, Major Incident, Change, Problem, Request and Knowledge capabilities where licensed. Avoid duplicating native record authority; extend only to address an evidenced gap.

Security, promotion and assurance

Separate custom application scopes, use least-privilege ACLs, preserve audit trails, require versioned tests and signed approvals, and verify the target release before describing functionality as installed.

Licensing and entitlements

The static portfolio and its local reviewer selector require no ServiceNow licence. Live custom Workbench deployment may require App Engine/custom app rights and role/ACL privileges. ITSM/MIM, ITOM/Discovery, CMDB products, IntegrationHub spokes, Performance Analytics, AI, Security Operations and external Jira/Notion/Confluence provider access have feature- and contract-specific entitlements; evaluate each in the target subscription.
