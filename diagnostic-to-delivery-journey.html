ServiceNow · architecture integration

From diagnostic evidence to governed delivery.

Two complementary Workbench architectures: investigate and review findings first; then translate approved opportunities into traceable delivery. This page describes an architectural handoff, not a proven end-to-end live integration.

SOURCE-ONLY architecture No production verification

01 / The client's pain & history

In a representative ITIL-aligned enterprise, service improvement work often begins with disconnected interviews, platform exports and issue registers. A team may identify configuration risks but lose the rationale as work moves into requirements, design, build and handover. The resulting traceability gap makes prioritisation, approval and operational acceptance harder. This is an illustrative scenario, not an assertion about a particular customer.

03 / Architectural best practices

OOTB-first

Use native ITSM records and platform capabilities where licensed and suitable; avoid creating parallel incident, problem or change processes merely to support the portfolio architecture.

Least privilege

Keep diagnostic collectors read-only, restrict access by role, and exclude credentials and customer identifiers from public artefacts.

Governed traceability

Maintain references from evidence to reviewed findings, approved requirements, test outcomes and release decisions. Validate mappings before automating transfer.
