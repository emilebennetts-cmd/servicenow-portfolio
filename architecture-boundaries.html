Handoff and control model

  1. Diagnostic captures operational observations and findings under its own governance.
  2. Delivery receives references for review, workshop decisions and traceable implementation planning; it does not silently mutate diagnostic evidence.
  3. Controlled build and acceptance require target-specific identity, source revision, permissions, test execution and reviewer evidence.
  4. Production claims require separate authorised production validation. None are asserted here.

Screenshot and confidentiality gate

Do not publish original instance captures until provenance, customer neutrality, secret/PII redaction, explicit approval and file-integrity checks are complete. No screenshots on this page are represented as verified ServiceNow captures.

Licensing and entitlement dependencies

The static public portfolio requires no ServiceNow entitlement. Running scoped applications requires authorised instance access and appropriate custom-app rights. ITOM/Discovery, IntegrationHub spokes, Performance Analytics, AI capabilities and third-party providers may require separately contracted licences or subscriptions depending on the actual deployed feature set. Verify contractual entitlement before enabling any integration or collector.
