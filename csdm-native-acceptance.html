G01 / MODEL

Release, plugins and class metadata

Pin the target release and validate classes, relationship types, installed capabilities and permitted customisations.

G02 / IDENTITY

IRE identification & source precedence

Validate stable identifiers, reconciliation authority and positive/negative collision fixtures with observable decisions.

G03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Attested business & technical owners

Separate real owner attestation from inferred assignment and exercise the null-owner negative path.

G04 / TOPOLOGY

Approved CI relationship evidence

Check supported direction and relationship types, traced paths, unmatched edges and evidence provenance; never infer sys_ids.

G05 / HEALTH

Complete and bounded health measures

Reconcile with CMDB Health, record denominators/exclusions, nulls, aggregate caps and unavailable measurements.

G06 / CONTROL

Approved change, rollback & rescan

Prepare reproducible fixtures, ACL checks, bounded reruns, rollback and independent reviewer evidence tied to source version.

Immutable safety boundary: exported records are MOCK-ONLY / UNREVIEWED / NOT VERIFIED / HOLD, even when all six preparation stages are “staged.” Browser exercise is not DEV-VERIFIED, PRODUCTION-VERIFIED, a live CMDB query, a human approval, or an entitlement assessment.

03 / Architectural best practices

OOTB first, with explicit entitlements and limits

Prefer supported platform controls

Use CI Class Manager, Identification and Reconciliation Engine, CMDB Health, CSDM and Service Portfolio Management rather than a bespoke duplicate engine or assumed service hierarchy.

Protect the evidence envelope

Use synthetic fixtures here. In native DEV, log source SHA, instance/release alias, executing role, case ID, expected/actual, absence/partial states, retention and independently reviewed receipts. Keep customer identifiers in controlled storage.

Licence and plugin checks

This static site needs no ServiceNow licence. Native scope depends on subscription and roles for CMDB/IRE/Health, SPM, Discovery/Service Mapping, Service Graph, IntegrationHub, App Engine/custom tables, ATF and third-party systems.
