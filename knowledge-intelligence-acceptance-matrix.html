01 · The client's pain & history

A knowledge catalogue is not an operational diagnosis.

In a representative enterprise, a support team inherits questions, partial queries and scattered troubleshooting notes across many licensed ServiceNow products. Finding a plausible rule in a catalogue does not mean its table exists, the operator has access, its results are complete or a recommendation is approved.

Source discrepancy caught in review: the companion README lists 16 tables in its update-set template, while its PDI verification checklist calls for 17. The exact installed schema and record count must be reconciled in the target instance. Neither document is treated as an authoritative runtime read-back.

Release boundary: even a fully checked fixture is only REVIEW-READY · HOLD. Client acceptance, DEV verification and production authorisation require independent, dated evidence and authorised sign-off. No original Workbench screenshots, credentials, customer data or private source scripts are embedded.




Licensing and entitlement dependencies
The static website and synthetic checklist require no ServiceNow licence. Native implementation requires authorised custom application/scope and ACL rights; each collector additionally requires access to its underlying product tables. ITSM, ITOM/Discovery, SecOps, IRM, CSM, HRSD, ITAM, SPM, WSD, App Engine, AI/Virtual Agent and IntegrationHub functionality may require separate licences or installed plugins, depending on the contracted instance. Knowledge-import rights and external API/provider terms require independent confirmation.


Acceptance should follow least privilege, OOTB-first platform decisions, upgrade-safe metadata and ITIL-aligned controlled change. Source evidence and the September DEV report do not establish production verification or permission to publish screenshot originals.
