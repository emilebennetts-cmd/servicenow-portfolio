01 · The client's pain & history

Incomplete observations can masquerade as healthy systems.

An illustrative enterprise receives fragmented ITSM, CMDB, SecOps and ITOM extracts. One collector returns no rows because its product is absent; another is limited to a subset of groups. Without applicability, data provenance and explicit review states, teams can mistake missing evidence for no problems and prematurely raise remediation. This is a representative scenario, not a verified customer incident.

Coverage gap

The assessment cannot distinguish in-scope checks from features not installed or not permitted.

False certainty

Unknown denominators and capped results distort rates, duplicates and risk prioritisation.

Handoff gap

Drafted findings may lack peer review, reproducibility and change authority.

03 · Architectural best practices
Native baseline before a bespoke collector result.


OOTB-first
Compare CMDB observations with supported CMDB Health completeness, correctness, compliance and relationship indicators. Confirm job settings, class filters and source coverage.


Explicit unknowns
Differentiate zero from not installed, not assessed, stale, truncated and permission denied. Do not infer zero defects from an unknown denominator.


Least privilege and scale
Validate scripts, cost of aggregate/grouped queries, data minimisation and product access. Aggregated descriptive values can still be personally identifying.


Acceptance requires product applicability; version and role evidence; negative ACL tests; controlled sample and scale checks; DIAG schema and privacy review; denominator reconciliation; human finding sign-off; change and rollback authorisation. A repository README describes historical 1 September 2026 DEV testing, but this site adds no new independently observed DEV or production execution.


Licensing and entitlements: no ServiceNow licence for the public static page. Native collectors require approved access and relevant installed product entitlements. ITSM, Discovery/ITOM, SecOps, IRM, CSM, HRSD, HAM/SAM, IntegrationHub and external provider licences vary by contract and feature; verify scope before running anything.
