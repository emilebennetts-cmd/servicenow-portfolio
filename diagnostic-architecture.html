01 / The client's pain & history

When every investigation starts from a blank page.

Platform teams inherit inconsistent assessments: scattered scripts, undocumented findings, limited evidence traceability and remediation recommendations that can be difficult to review. In complex ServiceNow estates, a technically correct observation is not enough—it needs a repeatable method and defensible proof.

This is a representative operating challenge, not a claim about a named customer's environment.

Engineering objectives

Standardise investigation steps; keep data collection read-only; reject unsafe evidence; make rule outcomes explainable; preserve human review; and create reusable reports and delivery artefacts.

03 / Architectural best practices

Safety and governance by design.

Read-only boundary

Collector sources use read/aggregate operations; the workspace does not execute scripts against the instance. The companion lint rejects mutation calls.

Controlled evidence intake

Evidence envelopes are validated before rule evaluation. The source documents secret scanning, consumer-data refusal and measured evidence strings.

Human decision rights

Correlation does not prove causation. Findings remain reviewable and solutions are explicitly marked DRAFT until accepted.

Local-first trade-off

The primary interface uses an IndexedDB local store and does not provide an automatic live instance connection. Browser-local data requires explicit retention and export procedures.

Separation of concerns

Keep diagnostic investigation separate from Delivery Workbench's engagement and implementation workflow; transfer reviewed findings through governed handoff references.

Upgrade-conscious deployment

Build from versioned Now SDK source, validate offline, install only into authorised non-production environments and promote through formal release approvals.
