01 / The client's pain & history

When investigation ends, delivery starts again from zero.

FRAGMENTATION

Evidence gets stranded

Findings and supporting observations live in investigation records, while delivery teams start fresh discovery and manually restate the issue.

REWORK

Known solutions are rebuilt

Similar ITSM, CMDB and platform-control issues produce repeated workshop questions, designs, acceptance criteria and test plans.

CONTROL GAP

Traceability fades

When a recommendation becomes a change, the original evidence, risk decision and approved pattern version may not travel with it.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

One traceable handoff, two bounded applications.

Diagnostics remains responsible for investigation. Delivery remains responsible for engagement execution. A reviewed, minimal handoff contract links them without making either workbench the master of the other's records.

03 / Architectural best practices

A minimal, governable integration contract.

The following fields are a design proposal, not a claim about existing tables or a deployed API. Use approved platform APIs or scoped IntegrationHub patterns only after access and entitlements are confirmed.

Contract elementPurposeControl
source_finding_refImmutable diagnostic referenceResolve through authorised ACL-controlled lookup
evidence_refs[]Links to supporting evidenceReference only; do not copy secrets or client data
review_statusApproval to initiate deliveryReject unreviewed or superseded findings
risk_and_ownerAccountable remediation decisionRequire human ownership and risk acceptance
pattern_id + versionReproducible design baselineCertified/approved patterns only; pin version
correlation_keyIdempotent handoff and reconciliationPrevent duplicate engagements and replay
outcome_refTraceable release and acceptanceLink tests, change approval and handover

OOTB-first

Prefer native task, approval, change and knowledge mechanisms where appropriate. Avoid duplicating CMDB, incident, change or request system-of-record data.

Security by design

Use least-privilege scoped APIs, ACL checks, role separation, credential aliases, audit history and explicit data-classification controls.

Upgrade-conscious

Keep contracts versioned, test failure/retry paths, isolate integrations and require regression tests before promotion across environments.
