01 / Universal Delivery Workbench

From discovery to controlled handover

Legacy delivery frequently disperses requirements, design decisions, tests and handover across documents and disconnected tools. The workbench source describes a single governed journey that maintains evidence links and approval gates.

Discover & scope

Select a process or application scope; reconcile proposed requirements without overwriting reviewed answers.

Design & build

Use governed pattern candidates and a delivery plan; retain source provenance and review-required states.

Assure & hand over

Track acceptance, tests, release evidence and generated handover artefacts before closure.

02 / Diagnostic Workbench 2

Turn platform signals into governed findings

Operational investigations can become fragmented across manual checks and isolated reports. The Diagnostic Workbench source defines a scoped investigation UI, with a separately deployed Knowledge Intelligence companion containing read-only collectors and finding logic.

Collect

Use authorised, read-only collection boundaries; collection success must be proven per target instance.

Interpret

Associate findings with rules and evidence instead of presenting untraceable health scores.

Handoff

Reference reviewed diagnostic evidence from Delivery without creating a second writable evidence store.
