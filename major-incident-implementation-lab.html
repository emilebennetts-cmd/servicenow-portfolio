01 / The client's pain & history

When every outage starts with a different playbook

In a representative organisation, declaration criteria sit in spreadsheets, escalation clocks are disputed, emergency chat threads substitute for communication plans, and post-incident actions lack accountable owners. This is an illustrative delivery problem, not a claim about a named customer's baseline or measured outcome.

A governed MIM implementation records decisions once, connects them to the ServiceNow native incident lifecycle, and refuses to certify readiness when approval, access or evidence is missing.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

Three workshops; one governed implementation path

WORKSHOP 01

Identify & mobilise

Agree what qualifies as major, who declares, and when a candidate is proposed or promoted.

  • Impact, urgency and business-service criteria
  • Manager roles and ACLs
  • Trigger-rule review
WORKSHOP 02

Communicate & collaborate

Make communication, on-call mobilisation and external collaboration choices explicit.

  • Stakeholder updates and ownership
  • Incident command bridge
  • Provider approval and fallback
WORKSHOP 03

Restore, learn & measure

Define restoration authority and the post-incident review (PIR) handoff.

  • Restoration and closure criteria
  • PIR actions and evidence
  • ATF and negative-access tests

Licensing and entitlements
Confirm the contracted ITSM tier includes Major Incident Management (current packages can include Foundation, Advanced and Prime), and ensure relevant fulfiller/major_incident_manager roles and ACLs. Validate Service Operations Workspace, on-call, scoped App Engine/custom applications, IntegrationHub transactions/spokes, external collaboration providers, AI and analytics entitlements separately where used.


What makes a real DEV acceptance receipt?
Collect instance URL and platform release, installed scope/version, immutable source SHA and package digest, tester identity, expected and actual outcomes, audit evidence, negative-access results, time and change approval. A successful static webpage check or matching application version is not platform verification.


Explicit integration and publication boundaries
No calls to ServiceNow, no third-party messaging or Jira integration, no notification sending, no client data, credentials, private source, or genuine Workbench screenshot. Real captures remain on HOLD until image-specific provenance, irreversible redaction and publication approval.
