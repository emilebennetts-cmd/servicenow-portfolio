## 3. Architectural best practices, validation and entitlements

A review derivative must be re-inspected for residual pixels, metadata, account/engagement identifiers, cross-scope permissions and client data. Explicit asset-specific permission, approved caption and a byte-bound manifest are mandatory before any public image appears. Current installed versions and behaviour must be independently verified under authorised least-privilege DEV access; screenshots alone do not prove implementation, persistence, permissions or Production readiness.

The public portfolio and browser-local review need **no ServiceNow licence**. Native Delivery/Diagnostic execution and acceptance depend on scoped-app hosting rights and platform roles/ACLs. Additional ITSM, ITOM/Discovery, Service Graph Connectors, IntegrationHub, SecOps, IRM, ITAM, analytics, AI/spokes or other product entitlements are target- and use-case-specific.
