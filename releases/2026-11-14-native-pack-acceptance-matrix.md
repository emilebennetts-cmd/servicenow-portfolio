## 1. The client's pain and history

A historical synthetic Incident pack pilot described successful draft generation and limited native read-back, but the broader, reconciled source candidate has a separate 29-case acceptance plan. Treating the historical pilot and later planned verification as equivalent would hide unresolved negative-access, isolation, rollback and release gates. The 83/83 dated metadata read-back observation is not a replacement for executing the new matrix.

## 2. ServiceNow solution delivered here

- Added a public-safe, responsive **29-card case catalogue**, preserving trace IDs NV-001 through NV-029 and paraphrasing the private 21 October 2026 validation matrix into non-sensitive acceptance summaries.
- Added two keyboard-accessible, labelled filters for source-defined workstreams and test personas. Results include an accessible live count. All 29 cards remain readable when JavaScript is disabled.
- Added **local CSV download** of the filtered, source-only rehearsal subset. Every exported row is explicitly **NOT RUN**, `runtime_verified=false`, `release_authorised=false`; users cannot record or simulate a pass.
- Enhanced the existing pilot hero link without altering the prior six-step timeline, six historical evidence rows or their claim boundaries.
- Registered a separate **SOURCE-ONLY** claim alongside the previous **DEV-REPORTED** historical pilot, and reconciled its Portfolio Explorer badge to **MIXED CLAIMS** instead of promoting either.
- Added offline structural/publication regression checks and four-viewport Chromium acceptance checks for filters, empty state, mobile overflow and downloaded CSV semantics.

### Workstream coverage

Prepared gates cover identity/preflight, metadata, access, cross-engagement isolation, guarded record provenance, draft generation, replay/persistence, invalid input, held-capability refusal, scope/entitlement choices, concurrency, synthetic failure recovery, storage integrity, lifecycle state, regression, no outward effects, source preservation and independent acceptance.

All substantive test outcomes require the authorised owner and reviewer to execute the **controlled private procedures** on an approved non-production instance. A public static page cannot perform those actions.

## 3. Architecture, safety and assurance

The source 21 October 2026 pack and its blank execution record were reviewed from the restricted Delivery repository; the document states the candidate is unreleased and not installed by the validation pack. The private Diagnostic repository and the controlled fictional completion runbook were also reviewed to preserve independent gate authority and the separate Workbench boundary. Neither private raw procedure text nor any instance identifier, source payload hash, screenshot, customer data or Drive link is embedded in the website.

The independent approval route remains: confirm target instance and entitlements; reconcile exact candidate/build hashes; review dependencies, preview and before-state; approve any change; perform read-only metadata verification; execute role and negative-access checks using isolated personas and synthetic engagements; confirm idempotency and recovery; then review release and image-specific approvals. Metadata-only evidence never establishes complete functional or production assurance.

**Licensing and entitlements:** The static portfolio and exported CSV require **no ServiceNow licence**. Native application installation may require App Engine/custom-table entitlements and authorised scope roles/ACLs, platform admin approval, and applicable ITSM, ITOM, IntegrationHub, AI, analytics and third-party rights. Confirm contracted rights for the exact target; neither source presence nor a pilot implies entitlement.
