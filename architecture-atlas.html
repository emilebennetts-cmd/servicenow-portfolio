Engineering controls

What makes the architecture defensible?

Traceability first

Maintain provenance from observations and requirements through design, tests and handover. Avoid presenting draft pattern candidates as certified solutions.

Least privilege

Keep the offline, zero-footprint consulting route separate from authenticated instance collection and approved deployment.

Upgrade-aware

Prefer supported ServiceNow OOTB processes and APIs, protect scoped application identities and gate promotion with testing and review.

Licensing and entitlements

The static portfolio requires no ServiceNow licence. Runtime custom applications may require App Engine/custom-app rights; ITSM, ITOM, Performance Analytics, IntegrationHub/spokes, AI capabilities and external provider subscriptions must be evaluated against the target instance and actual feature usage. No entitlement is assumed from source code.
