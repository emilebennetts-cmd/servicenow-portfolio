01 / The client's pain & history

Healthy-looking counts can conceal unsafe decisions.

Multi-source CMDBs accumulate duplicate identities, mismatched lifecycle states, stale discovery observations and broken relationships. A count on a dashboard can point to an investigation, but it cannot identify the authoritative source, demonstrate business impact, or authorise a destructive fix. Historical remediation efforts often fail when an engineer merges CIs before checking IRE rules, reconciliation precedence, service dependencies and downstream task references.

Identity confusion

Duplicate names or serials are investigation candidates. They are not automatic evidence that two CIs represent the same asset.

Incomplete measurements

Unavailable tables, capped groupings and overlapping counts must be reported explicitly; they are not green health outcomes.

Governance gap

Unowned remediation can invalidate relationships, asset linkage, incident history or service-mapping paths.

02 / The ServiceNow solution

Four gates between detection and authorised change.

The architecture uses read-only health collection for initial signals, CMDB ownership and IRE configuration review for diagnosis, risk-managed change and dependency analysis for control, then independently observed native read-back for closure.

G1 — DetectCollect aggregate quality signals; disclose completeness and group caps.
G2 — AttributeCompare class, identifiers, data source and reconciliation authority.
G3 — AuthoriseReview CI dependencies, ownership, risk, CAB policy and rollback.
G4 — VerifyRead back CI/relationship state and service impact on the target build.

Boundary: No CI is changed in this website. Selecting “approved” in the synthetic fixture is not a ServiceNow change approval, technical sign-off or permission to perform a merge.

Evidence basis and limitations

This narrative was informed by review of the private Diagnostic Workbench collector families for duplicates, IRE, relationships and stale lifecycle data, plus controlled Delivery As-Built and Diagnostic runbook guidance. No source code, proprietary mappings or original screenshots were copied.

Historical September source/DEV baselines and newer repository source versions remain distinct. The interactive rules and all values are synthetic, not a recorded ServiceNow production incident or verified deployed integration.
